Giant Cookie Recipe

This giant cookie recipe is a great way to provide a temporary
sugar high whilst endulging in a delicious treat!

  1. Preheat oven to 180C and line two baking trays with baking paper
  2. Melt butter, then add Chelsea White Sugar and Chelsea Soft Brown Sugar and cream these together. Beat in the eggs and vanilla then use a spoon or spatula to mix in the dry ingredients (including the chocolate chips).
  3. Roll into medium sized balls and place on prepared trays. They shouldn't spread too much so flatten slightly. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until lightly browned around the edges.
  4. Leave the chocolate chip cookies to cool on trays for a couple of minutes, before transferring to a wire cooling rack.
